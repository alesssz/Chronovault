# 1. Features

| Area | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Accounts** | Sign-up, sign-in and sign-out with email and password. Each user only sees their own tracks and playlists. **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page emails a link to choose a new one; the link works once, in any browser (the phone that opens the email, too). |
| **Library** | Track list with cover, composers, duration, genre (the most specific name: the subgenre when set, the whole `Metal › Thrash Metal` on hover), mastering status, synced-lyrics (orange when the sync is to be checked after a replaced audio) and visibility icons (genre and icons in columns, from tablet width), and a **Draft** badge on work-in-progress versions. Search (title, composers, album), genre, **subgenre** (disabled until a genre is chosen, then that genre's subgenres your tracks use), mastering and version (final / drafts) filters, sorting. **Thousands of tracks scroll as lightly as twenty**: only the rows on screen are drawn. **Select** mode: tick several tracks to mark them final, add them to a playlist, share them for feedback or delete them in one go (on phones the actions sit in a bar at the bottom of the screen, each named under its icon; from tablet width the bar stays under the header, with short names (Final, Playlist, Feedback) up to 1024 px and the full ones above). On touch screens a **long press** on a track starts it with that track ticked, with a short vibration, and the ✕ at the start of the bar ends it. Clicking a track's name plays it; clicking the name of the track already in the player opens the full-screen view. |
| **Upload** | Drop several files at once (MP3, WAV, FLAC, AAC/M4A, OGG/Opus, WebM; max 500 MB each). Title, composers, album, year, genre, lyrics (synced ones too) and cover are read from the tags; a genre tag names a genre, or a subgenre and so its genre too ("Thrash Metal" is Metal › Thrash Metal), and a name the lists do not have leaves the track without a genre. **WAV files are converted to FLAC losslessly**, verified bit for bit ([7.4](07-data-flows.md#74-lossless-wav--flac-conversion)). Direct upload to storage with a progress bar, retried on network errors. Leaving the page (or closing it) while files are still uploading asks first. |
| **Player** | Bottom bar that stays visible across pages: on phones cover, title, play and next; from tablet width the waveform sits above the controls in the middle of the bar (the same order as the full-screen view), between the track and the volume. Clickable and draggable waveform, elapsed and remaining time, play/pause, stop, previous/next, **shuffle** and **repeat** (off / all / one), volume and mute (volume, shuffle and repeat are remembered). Lock-screen and media-key controls (Media Session). **The volume slider is for a mouse or trackpad** (in a popover in a narrow window); on **touch screens**, phones and tablets alike, the hardware buttons own the volume: the player offers mute alone and always plays at full level, so it never adds a second, hidden level to theirs. **Touch gestures** on the bar's track area: swipe left for the next track, right for the previous one, up for the full-screen view. **One track follows another at once**: the next one is asked for a little before the end, so it starts without waiting for the network. Playback goes on with the screen off; the lock screen and the media notification show the track (cover, title, composers) or, where it has none, the Chronovault artwork and name. **Cast** to a TV or speaker on the Wi-Fi network (Chromecast / Google TV from Android, AirPlay from iPhone and iPad): the button is always there on phones and tablets (a tap searches the network), and on a desktop appears when a device is around. Bluetooth speakers and headphones need nothing from the app: the phone sends all its audio there. |
| **Queue** | "Play next" and "Add to queue" in every track menu (library and playlists). The **Up next** panel (from the player bar, or the full-screen view on phones) shows the track playing and the upcoming ones: drag to reorder (mouse or touch), move up/down, remove, play one right away, clear; a long queue (a whole library) shows a hundred tracks at a time, **Show more** for the next hundred, while its heading counts them all. The queue is **saved on the device**: after a reload you find the same queue and track, paused where you left it. |
| **Full-screen player** | Large cover, waveform, controls and **synced lyrics**: the active line is highlighted (before the first one every line reads as plain text, and in the light theme the lines to come stay readable grey), the text scrolls by itself, clicking a line seeks there. Section headers (`[Verse 1]`, `[Chorus]`) are shown as titles. On touch screens, **pull it down to close** it. The ✕ of its top bar **closes the player** altogether (on phones the only way: their bar has no room for it); share pages leave it out. From tablet width it is the share page's body itself (`TrackShowcase`): a centred band with the title, the cover and the description on the left, the lyrics **centred** in a panel as tall beside them, the controls in a bar at the bottom; a track without lyrics shows its **description** in that panel instead, and with neither the view is one centred column. On short phones, and on short laptop screens (1280×720, 1366×768), the cover shrinks with the window's height, so the controls and the volume are always on screen (the share page of a track does the same for its Play button). On phones the lyrics have **a view of their own**: the normal view shows cover, info, waveform, controls and the track's **description**; the **Lyrics** button of its top bar swaps them for a small cover and title, the lyrics filling the screen (bigger, scrolling in their own box) and the waveform with previous / play / next at the bottom; while it plays there, the **screen stays on**. The choice is remembered on the device. |
| **Editor** | Title, composers, album, year, **genre** (one of a fixed list of 19 main genres, "Other" for what fits none) and **subgenre** (disabled until a genre is chosen; that genre's subgenres, over 400 built in, or a new one typed in, created as yours if it is coherent with the genre: not a genre's name, nor another genre's subgenre, "Thrash Metal belongs to Metal"), description, Suno prompt, mastered yes/no, draft/final version, visibility (private / unlisted / public), cover (you choose the square in a crop editor — drag, zoom with wheel/pinch/slider — then it is converted to WebP), lyrics with section headers. Unsaved changes are never lost by accident: leaving the page, reloading it or closing the tab asks first. Going to another page of the app asks in the app's own dialog, which can also **save and leave**. |
| **Replace audio** | In the editor's **File** section: a new audio file for the track, such as its **master**, keeping everything else (title and all the metadata, lyrics, cover, plays, votes, playlists, share links). Only the file changes, and what is measured from it: format, size, duration, waveform; a WAV is converted to FLAC as on upload, and the new file's tags are ignored. The old file is deleted once the new one is in place; if anything fails, the track keeps its old file. A track playing moves to the new file at the same second. **Synced lyrics are kept but flagged for checking**, as a master may trim or shift the audio: the Sync tab says "Audio file replaced" (with a **Check** badge on the tab) and the library shows the synced-lyrics icon in orange, until you press **Sync checked** ([7.19](07-data-flows.md#719-replacing-a-tracks-audio)). |
| **Lyrics sync** | "Tap to sync": while the track plays, press <kbd>Space</kbd> (or tap **Stamp line**) at the start of each line; on phones Stamp line and Undo stay pinned above the player bar, each tap gives a short **vibration** (Android), and the **screen stays on** while syncing. Playback at 0.5× / 0.75× with the pitch preserved, a zoomable detailed waveform with a **draggable marker per line** (each with its line on a dark label, cut before the next marker), replay of the selected line (<kbd>R</kbd>), ±0.05 s nudges (<kbd>←</kbd> <kbd>→</kbd>), undo (<kbd>↑</kbd>) (while syncing these keys are the editor's: the player's own shortcuts wait), **LRC** import (paste or open an `.lrc` file) and export (copy or download `.lrc`). All manual: no automatic alignment. |
| **Draft versions** | New uploads are **drafts** (e.g. one of several Suno versions of a song); the chosen one is marked **final** (track menu, edit page, or several at once). Select 2-3 drafts → **Share for feedback** creates an unlisted **group of versions** ("Night drive – versions") and copies its link: friends hear every version in a row, marked **Work in progress**. On the group's page each version's title takes up to two lines, so on phones the part that tells versions apart is not cut off. Friends **pick their favourite** with a heart, optionally with their name and a comment, no account needed. Groups have their own **Versions** page, apart from the playlists: a **dot on "Versions"** and a **"new" badge** on a group's card say that friends voted or commented since your last visit; open one to see the **votes, plays and comments** per version (the new ones marked), copy its link again or mark the chosen version final, which **closes the voting**. Then delete the others in one go ([7.17](07-data-flows.md#717-draft-versions-and-feedback)). |
| **Playlists** | Ordered lists of your tracks (groups of versions are on the Versions page instead). Create them from the Playlists page or from a track's menu ("Add to playlist" → "New playlist…"), reorder by dragging (mouse or touch) or with Move up/down, remove tracks, play them in sequence. A playlist's page starts with its tracks, its description under the title; title, description and visibility change in **Edit details** (a dialog). Cover: an automatic 2×2 mosaic of the first tracks' covers, or an image you upload. |
| **Download** | "Download" in the track menus and on the edit page (and, for visitors, on the share pages) saves the audio file **with its metadata written in**: title, composers (as artist and composer), album, year, genre (the most specific name: the subgenre when set, read back to both on upload), description (as comment), lyrics, **synced lyrics** and cover. FLAC and MP3 get every field; WAV the text fields; other formats are saved as stored. The audio itself is untouched, and the Suno prompt and mastering status are never written. |
| **Sharing** | Unlisted and public **tracks** get a public page, `/t/<track id>`: cover, title, composers, album, year, genre (the subgenre when set), description, lyrics and a player. Shared **playlists** get `/p/<playlist id>`: a small cover beside the title, the description, then the track list in the first screen, the player a bar at the bottom of the window from tablet width (as on the track page, with previous / next and full screen), sequential playback, and the lyrics of the track being played (a second click on its row opens it full screen, with its description). From tablet width the track page is the same as the library's full-screen view (one component, `TrackShowcase`): a centred band, the title on its own line and composers · album under it, a big square cover and the description (**About**) on the left, the lyrics **centred** on the right in a panel exactly as tall as that column (the description takes their place for a track without lyrics); the player is a **bar at the bottom of the window** with **Download** (no full-screen button there: the full-screen view has the same layout; phones keep it). A click on the cover opens it **full screen**. Their player is the waveform with one row of controls under it, play in the middle: **previous / next** around it (playlists), the **volume** (mute alone on touch screens), the **full-screen view** with the synced lyrics (and the phones' lyrics view), and on phones a **mini player** at the bottom of the screen once the page's player is scrolled away; playback goes on **with the screen off**, from one track to the next. No account needed to open them. Visitors can **download** unlisted tracks (and the private ones of a shared playlist, there) with the same tags as the owner's download; **public tracks never**, the track's own visibility deciding, not the playlist's. "Copy share link" in the menus and on the edit pages; on phones and tablets it is **"Share link"** and opens the device's share sheet (WhatsApp, Telegram, mail…). The Suno prompt and the mastering status are **never** shared. **Sharing a playlist shares all its tracks, private ones included** (to listen and to download, only inside the playlist; the editor warns about them). Link previews in chat apps and social networks show the title and the cover, or the Chronovault artwork for an item without one, under the site name "Chronovault". |
| **Statistics** | Account menu → Statistics: how many times your **share links** (`/t/…`, `/p/…`) were opened in the last 7 or 30 days, per day and per track or playlist, with where visitors came from (WhatsApp, Instagram…), their country and device: real data from **Vercel Web Analytics** on the deployed site, clearly flagged **demo data** locally. Next to them, how many times visitors really **listened** (30 seconds, or half a shorter track) and how many **to the end**, counted by the app itself, so real everywhere; **your own plays** are shown apart and never inflate the numbers; on phones each page's numbers go under its title ([7.16](07-data-flows.md#716-statistics)). |
| **Metadata export and import** | Account menu → Export & import metadata: **every track's metadata in one file**, and many tracks filled in from one, in **Excel, CSV or JSON**, each row finding its track by **title**. The download holds every field (lyrics, description and Suno prompt included; not the synced lyrics): it is the export and the template alike. Change what you want and upload it: the page shows what changes, track by track, and which rows it skips and why, before anything is saved; the file imported back unchanged changes nothing. An empty cell leaves that field as it is. The genre must be one of the list; the subgenre one of its genre's, or a new name created as yours; anything else skips the row, with why. The Excel file lists the genres and subgenres on its Help sheet and turns a value it would refuse red as it is typed; the page has the same list, searchable, for CSV and JSON ([7.18](07-data-flows.md#718-exporting-and-importing-metadata)). |
| **Storage** | Account menu → Storage: how much audio and how many covers you store, and the **orphaned files** left by interrupted uploads, which can be deleted in one click (files younger than 24 hours are kept: an upload may still be in progress). |
| **Look** | The **Chronovault identity** ([5.6](05-architecture.md#56-brand-and-theme)): the logo (a gold portal crossed by three guitar strings, the middle one vibrating) with the name in Roman capitals, gold buttons and highlights, the spectral blue of the strings on the waveform and the progress lines, a night-blue background. Tracks and playlists without a cover show the **Chronovault artwork**, as do the lock screen and link previews; a playlist's artwork is blurred under its **initials** ("RT" for "Road trip"), so playlists without covers are told apart at a glance. |
| **Interface** | Responsive from phones to desktop, light and dark theme, checked by end-to-end tests at phone and tablet size. Slightly smaller text on phones (17 px instead of 20 px), so lists, filters and headers fit; there the track editor's Play and Download are icons, leaving the row to the title. Below 1024 px the editors (tracks, playlists, groups of versions) start with their form, the cover a small row above it, and end with details, sharing and Delete, so on a tablet the content has the whole width (beside the cover's column it kept about 400 px, and titles broke mid-word); below 768 px a **Save bar** at the bottom of the screen comes up while there are unsaved changes, in place of the header's Save. Below 1024 px the header's section links (Library, Playlists, Versions) are icons, their names in tooltips, so the header fits at tablet width; below 1280 px the library's search box has a row of its own above the filters (four across from 768 px). On phones the filters and the sort order sit in a **Filters** panel from the bottom of the screen, opened from beside the search box, whose button counts the active filters. While the search or a filter hides tracks, the library's subtitle says how many it shows ("12 of 24 tracks"). On touch screens only the track in the player has the play/pause overlay on its cover (there is no hover to reveal it on the others; a tap on the row plays). The **Android back button** closes the panel or dialog on top (full-screen player, Up next, dialogs) instead of leaving the page or the installed app. **Every icon-only button shows its name in a tooltip** on hover or keyboard focus (the same words screen readers announce); the drag grips say "Drag to reorder". |
| **Keyboard** | Player shortcuts on every page, the share pages included: <kbd>Space</kbd> play/pause, <kbd>←</kbd> / <kbd>→</kbd> five seconds back / forward, <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>←</kbd> / <kbd>→</kbd> previous / next track, <kbd>M</kbd> mute, <kbd>F</kbd> the full-screen view (and back), <kbd>?</kbd> their list (also in the account menu). They never take a key that means something else: typing, a focused button, a slider, a menu, a dialog, or the lyrics sync editor, which keeps its own keys ([7.5](07-data-flows.md#75-playback)). |
| **PWA** | Installable on phones and desktop as **Chronovault** (manifest, icons, service worker for static assets). The installed icon is the full logo on a night-blue tile; Android's adaptive icon keeps it whole whatever shape the system crops. |

**No AI.** The app uses no artificial-intelligence service, SDK or model. The "Suno prompt" field is just a text field to note down the prompt used elsewhere.
