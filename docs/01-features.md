# 1. Features

| Area | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Accounts** | Sign-up, sign-in and sign-out with email and password. Each user only sees their own tracks and playlists. |
| **Library** | Track list with cover, composers, duration, genre, mastering status, synced-lyrics and visibility badges, and a **Draft** badge on work-in-progress versions. Search (title, composers, album), genre, mastering and version (final / drafts) filters, sorting. **Thousands of tracks scroll as lightly as twenty**: only the rows on screen are drawn. **Select** mode: tick several tracks to mark them final, add them to a playlist, share them for feedback or delete them in one go. Clicking a track's name plays it; clicking the name of the track already in the player opens the full-screen view. |
| **Upload** | Drop several files at once (MP3, WAV, FLAC, AAC/M4A, OGG/Opus, WebM; max 500 MB each). Title, composers, album, year, genre, lyrics (synced ones too) and cover are read from the tags. **WAV files are converted to FLAC losslessly**, verified bit for bit ([7.4](07-data-flows.md#74-lossless-wav--flac-conversion)). Direct upload to storage with a progress bar, retried on network errors. Leaving the page (or closing it) while files are still uploading asks first. |
| **Player** | Bottom bar that stays visible across pages: on phones cover, title, play and next; from tablet width the waveform sits above the controls in the middle of the bar (the same order as the full-screen view), between the track and the volume. Clickable and draggable waveform, elapsed and remaining time, play/pause, stop, previous/next, **shuffle** and **repeat** (off / all / one), volume and mute (volume, shuffle and repeat are remembered). Lock-screen and media-key controls (Media Session). **The volume slider is for a mouse or trackpad** (in a popover in a narrow window); on **touch screens**, phones and tablets alike, the hardware buttons own the volume: the player offers mute alone and always plays at full level, so it never adds a second, hidden level to theirs. **Touch gestures** on the bar's track area: swipe left for the next track, right for the previous one, up for the full-screen view. **One track follows another at once**: the next one is asked for a little before the end, so it starts without waiting for the network. Playback goes on with the screen off; the lock screen and the media notification show the track (cover, title, composers) or, where it has none, the Chronovault artwork and name. **Cast** to a TV or speaker on the Wi-Fi network (Chromecast / Google TV from Android, AirPlay from iPhone and iPad): the button is always there on phones and tablets (a tap searches the network), and on a desktop appears when a device is around. Bluetooth speakers and headphones need nothing from the app: the phone sends all its audio there. |
| **Queue** | "Play next" and "Add to queue" in every track menu (library and playlists). The **Up next** panel (from the player bar, or the full-screen view on phones) shows the track playing and the upcoming ones: drag to reorder (mouse or touch), move up/down, remove, play one right away, clear; a long queue (a whole library) shows a hundred tracks at a time, **Show more** for the next hundred, while its heading counts them all. The queue is **saved on the device**: after a reload you find the same queue and track, paused where you left it. |
| **Full-screen player** | Large cover, waveform, controls and **synced lyrics**: the active line is highlighted, the text scrolls by itself, clicking a line seeks there. Section headers (`[Verse 1]`, `[Chorus]`) are shown as titles. On touch screens, **pull it down to close** it. On phones the lyrics have **a view of their own**: the normal view shows cover, info, waveform, controls and the track's **description**; the **Lyrics** button of its top bar swaps them for a small cover and title, the lyrics filling the screen (bigger, scrolling in their own box) and the waveform with previous / play / next at the bottom; while it plays there, the **screen stays on**. The choice is remembered on the device. |
| **Editor** | Title, composers, album, year, genre (built-in, or custom ones created on the fly), description, Suno prompt, mastered yes/no, draft/final version, visibility (private / unlisted / public), cover (you choose the square in a crop editor — drag, zoom with wheel/pinch/slider — then it is converted to WebP), lyrics with section headers. Unsaved changes are never lost by accident: leaving the page, reloading it or closing the tab asks first. |
| **Lyrics sync** | "Tap to sync": while the track plays, press <kbd>Space</kbd> (or tap **Stamp line**) at the start of each line; on phones Stamp line and Undo stay pinned above the player bar, each tap gives a short **vibration** (Android), and the **screen stays on** while syncing. Playback at 0.5× / 0.75× with the pitch preserved, a zoomable detailed waveform with a **draggable marker per line**, replay of the selected line (<kbd>R</kbd>), ±0.05 s nudges (<kbd>←</kbd> <kbd>→</kbd>), undo (<kbd>↑</kbd>) (while syncing these keys are the editor's: the player's own shortcuts wait), **LRC** import (paste or open an `.lrc` file) and export (copy or download `.lrc`). All manual: no automatic alignment. |
| **Draft versions** | New uploads are **drafts** (e.g. one of several Suno versions of a song); the chosen one is marked **final** (track menu, edit page, or several at once). Select 2-3 drafts → **Share for feedback** creates an unlisted **group of versions** ("Night drive – versions") and copies its link: friends hear every version in a row, marked **Work in progress**. Friends **pick their favourite** with a heart, optionally with their name and a comment, no account needed. Groups have their own **Versions** page, apart from the playlists: a **dot on "Versions"** and a **"new" badge** on a group's card say that friends voted or commented since your last visit; open one to see the **votes, plays and comments** per version (the new ones marked), copy its link again or mark the chosen version final, which **closes the voting**. Then delete the others in one go ([7.17](07-data-flows.md#717-draft-versions-and-feedback)). |
| **Playlists** | Ordered lists of your tracks (groups of versions are on the Versions page instead). Create them from the Playlists page or from a track's menu ("Add to playlist" → "New playlist…"), reorder by dragging (mouse or touch) or with Move up/down, remove tracks, play them in sequence. Cover: an automatic 2×2 mosaic of the first tracks' covers, or an image you upload. |
| **Download** | "Download" in the track menus and on the edit page saves the audio file **with its metadata written in**: title, composers (as artist and composer), album, year, genre, description (as comment), lyrics, **synced lyrics** and cover. FLAC and MP3 get every field; WAV the text fields; other formats are saved as stored. The audio itself is untouched, and the Suno prompt and mastering status are never written. |
| **Sharing** | Unlisted and public **tracks** get a public page, `/t/<track id>`: cover, title, composers, album, year, genre, description, lyrics and a player. Shared **playlists** get `/p/<playlist id>`: the track list, sequential playback, and the lyrics and description of the track being played. Their player has **previous / next** (playlists), the **volume** (mute alone on touch screens), the **full-screen view** with the synced lyrics (and the phones' lyrics view), and on phones a **mini player** at the bottom of the screen once the page's player is scrolled away; playback goes on **with the screen off**, from one track to the next. No account needed to open them. "Copy share link" in the menus and on the edit pages; on phones and tablets it is **"Share link"** and opens the device's share sheet (WhatsApp, Telegram, mail…). The Suno prompt and the mastering status are **never** shared. **Sharing a playlist shares all its tracks, private ones included** (only inside the playlist; the editor warns about them). Link previews in chat apps and social networks show the title and the cover, or the Chronovault artwork for an item without one, under the site name "Chronovault". |
| **Statistics** | Account menu → Statistics: how many times your **share links** (`/t/…`, `/p/…`) were opened in the last 7 or 30 days, per day and per track or playlist, with where visitors came from (WhatsApp, Instagram…), their country and device: real data from **Vercel Web Analytics** on the deployed site, clearly flagged **demo data** locally. Next to them, how many times visitors really **listened** (30 seconds, or half a shorter track) and how many **to the end**, counted by the app itself, so real everywhere; **your own plays** are shown apart and never inflate the numbers ([7.16](07-data-flows.md#716-statistics)). |
| **Storage** | Account menu → Storage: how much audio and how many covers you store, and the **orphaned files** left by interrupted uploads, which can be deleted in one click (files younger than 24 hours are kept: an upload may still be in progress). |
| **Look** | The **Chronovault identity** ([5.6](05-architecture.md#56-brand-and-theme)): the logo (a gold portal crossed by three guitar strings, the middle one vibrating) with the name in Roman capitals, gold buttons and highlights, the spectral blue of the strings on the waveform and the progress lines, a night-blue background. Tracks and playlists without a cover show the **Chronovault artwork**, as do the lock screen and link previews. |
| **Interface** | Responsive from phones to desktop, light and dark theme, checked by end-to-end tests at phone and tablet size. Slightly smaller text on phones (17 px instead of 20 px), so lists, filters and headers fit; there the track editor's Play, Download and Save are icons, leaving the row to the title. On touch screens the play button on track covers is always shown (there is no hover to reveal it). The **Android back button** closes the panel or dialog on top (full-screen player, Up next, dialogs) instead of leaving the page or the installed app. **Every icon-only button shows its name in a tooltip** on hover or keyboard focus (the same words screen readers announce); the drag grips say "Drag to reorder". |
| **Keyboard** | Player shortcuts on every page, the share pages included: <kbd>Space</kbd> play/pause, <kbd>←</kbd> / <kbd>→</kbd> five seconds back / forward, <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>←</kbd> / <kbd>→</kbd> previous / next track, <kbd>M</kbd> mute, <kbd>F</kbd> the full-screen view (and back), <kbd>?</kbd> their list (also in the account menu). They never take a key that means something else: typing, a focused button, a slider, a menu, a dialog, or the lyrics sync editor, which keeps its own keys ([7.5](07-data-flows.md#75-playback)). |
| **PWA** | Installable on phones and desktop as **Chronovault** (manifest, icons, service worker for static assets). The installed icon is the full logo on a night-blue tile; Android's adaptive icon keeps it whole whatever shape the system crops. |

**No AI.** The app uses no artificial-intelligence service, SDK or model. The "Suno prompt" field is just a text field to note down the prompt used elsewhere.
